Repeatless — Privacy Policy

Effective date: 27 July 2026

The short version: your recordings stay on your phone. Always. If you turn on the optional Auto‑Parent AI feature, only text (never audio) is sent to Google's Gemini API to pick which of your own clips to play.

Your recordings stay on your device

All audio clips, labels, and soundboards you create in Repeatless are stored only on your device, in the app's private storage. They are never uploaded to us or to anyone else. Repeatless has no user accounts and no server that stores your data, and Android's automatic cloud backup is switched off for the app so your recordings are not copied to Google Drive either.

Microphone

The microphone is used for two things only: (1) recording your own clips, and (2) if you turn on the optional Auto‑Parent AI feature, transcribing nearby speech to text on your device using your phone's built-in speech recognition. Captured audio is processed locally and discarded; it is never transmitted.

Auto-Parent AI (optional, off by default)

When you enable Auto‑Parent AI, the on-device text transcript and the labels of your clips are sent to Google's Gemini API so the app can choose which of your clips to play. Your audio recordings — and your children's voices — are never sent. This text processing is subject to Google's API terms. Because transcripts may include things said by children in your home, the feature is opt-in, runs only while you switch it on, shows a visible status while listening, and stores no transcripts after matching.

What we collect

Nothing is collected or stored off your device by Repeatless. No analytics identifiers, no advertising trackers, no accounts.

Purchases

The optional Pro unlock is processed entirely by Google Play. We never see your payment details.

Backups

The backup feature writes a file to a location you choose. That file contains your recordings — treat it like any personal file. Restoring or deleting it is entirely under your control.

Your control

You can delete any clip or board in the app at any time. Uninstalling Repeatless removes all locally stored data. There are no server copies to request or erase.

Children

Repeatless is made for parents and adults, not for use by children. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children; audio never leaves the device, and Auto‑Parent transcripts are used transiently for matching and not stored.

Changes & contact

Repeatless is published by Prime Paints (South Africa), which is the controller of any data described above. If our data practices ever change, this policy will be updated here before the change ships.
